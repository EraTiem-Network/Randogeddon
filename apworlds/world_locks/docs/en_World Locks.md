# World Locks

Some worlds of the multiworld start locked and open once their key is found in another world.

This game has no locations and nobody plays it. Each locked world gets one key item ("World Key 1", "World Key 2",
...), placed somewhere else in the multiworld; to make room for the keys, as many filler items of the other worlds are
taken out of the item pool. Nothing in a locked world is reachable for the generator until its key is found, so the seed
stays completable. A multiworld has at most one World Locks slot, and at least one world must stay open.

See the setup guide for the options.
